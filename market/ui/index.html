About EdgeLane Matrix

Cut the noise. Get to the trade — fast. No flipping through ten charts. EdgeLane reads dealer GEX walls, bias, and live liquidity, then surfaces the few spreads with real structural edge, each ranked on one composite tradeability score (EV, health, liquidity, fill feasibility, PoP). Fully deterministic math — AI only narrates, never decides.

What makes it different

  • Decision to execution in one click. Setup looks right → fire the full multi-leg spread to a connected broker instantly. On SPX/NDX the level is gone in 30–60s — EdgeLane gives that time back to analysis and kills the fat-finger errors of hand-keying four legs.
  • No supported broker? Still instant. One click copies the ticket to paste into your own broker's app — same speed, same accuracy edge.
  • Dealer-GEX walls with strength tiers — see where price pins and where it runs.
  • One honest score per spread, with fill feasibility built in — no wall of unranked chains.
  • Bias accuracy tracked over time, so the engine stays accountable to its own calls.

How not to use it

  • Not financial advice or a guaranteed outcome — it's a probabilistic edge.
  • Don't override liquidity flags and chase a spread you can't exit.
  • Don't oversize on a high score — it ranks edge, not position size.

Options trading carries substantial risk. A research and decision-support tool, not investment advice.
