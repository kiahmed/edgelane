Simmer

A premium-selling readiness engine. It watches your tickers and tells you when a name is conditioned to sell a credit spread — and, far more often, why it isn't.

What Simmer is

Simmer sweeps your watchlist on a schedule and, for each name, draws one conclusion: either "sell this spread — here are the strikes, credit, POP and EV," or "no sellable spread — here is exactly what's blocking it." It is deliberately refusal-first: most names are vetoed most of the time, and the veto reasons are the product. A quiet dashboard is the system working, not a bug.

Every number is deterministic math over live option-chain data — expected value is payoff-integrated, probabilities come from the chain's own greeks, and liquidity/friction are measured in dollars. The LLM (Gemini) only writes prose and can pick a side or veto; it never promotes a score.

The one-line mental model

A name becomes sellable only when it clears 10 gates (5 hard-safety, 4 tunable, 1 "did any structure survive") and scores ≥ 70 on a 0–100 tradeability score. Miss a gate and there are no strikes to show — just the reason.

Reading a readiness card

Each ticker is one card. Cards start collapsed and stack by nearest expiry first; the header alone tells you the verdict, and you expand for the full evaluation.

Card anatomy

ElementWhat it tells you
Decision pill (READY / WATCH / VETOED) The one-word verdict. VETOED = a gate blocked it; WATCH = passed gates but scored below the ready band; READY = sellable now.
Expiry · DTEThe expiration under evaluation and days-to-expiration. A 📌 means you pinned the expiry (the engine isn't auto-picking).
Summary chip (the white box) Readable at a glance, even collapsed: SELL <short> / <long> when there's a target, else No sellable spread · x/10 vetoed (x = failed gates), or · below band when it passed gates but under-scored.
Gate checklistThe 10 gates as pass / fail / unknown. This leads — the score is secondary. "Unknown" means the engine couldn't evaluate that gate (missing data), not that you failed it.
Score barThe 0–100 tradeability score against the ready (70) and watch (50) bands, with confidence (data quality) and the market regime beneath it.
Trade block (only when READY)Structure, strikes, credit, max loss, POP, EV, alpha and the pre-set management plan (below).
⚠ soft warningsavoid_if notes — they inform, never veto (e.g. "premium is entirely earnings-driven", a regime suppressing a structure).

The trade block, field by field

Shown only when the name is READY — a lone underlying price is not a strike, so a vetoed card produces none.

FieldMeaning
credit — achievableThe package mid less realistic fill slippage, rounded down to the tick. This is what to actually expect to collect.
credit — advertisedThe raw package mid. You will rarely be filled here; the gap vs achievable is the honesty.
max lossWidth − credit, per share. The denominator for risk.
POPProbability the spread expires at/above breakeven under market IV. A second number "/ … fc" is the forecast-vol POP — the gap between them is the edge.
EVPayoff-integrated expected value per share at forecast vol.
alphaEV ÷ max loss — edge per unit of risk, as a percent.
manageThe exit is set before entry: take profit at 50% of max profit, actively manage at 21 DTE, stop at 2× the credit received.

Coming to a decision — the takeaway

  1. Read the pill first. VETOED → there is nothing to do; the chip's x/10 and the checklist say why. Move on.
  2. WATCH → conditions are fine but the edge isn't strong enough (score 50–69). Keep it on the list; it may cross into ready as vol/structure improve.
  3. READY → this is an actionable sell. Sanity-check the achievable credit (not advertised), confirm POP and alpha are worth it, and note the management plan is fixed in advance.
  4. Confidence & regime are context, not gates. A ready card with low confidence means the inputs were thin — weight it accordingly. A stressed regime down-weights or suppresses structures.

Decision bands: score ≥ 70 → ready · ≥ 50 → watch · below → avoid. Any hard gate failing overrides the score to vetoed.

The 10 gates, under the hood

Every name runs the same 10 gates, in this order. The first four (evaluated per symbol+expiry) and the fifth are Locked — hard safety rails you cannot switch off. Four are Tunable — thresholds you adjust in settings (within clamped bounds). The last checks that some concrete structure survived. A single failed gate is enough to veto; the card's x/10 counts failed gates (a gate can raise several reason tokens, but counts once). A gate whose only signal is an "unknown" token renders as unknown, not fail — meaning "couldn't evaluate", usually missing data.

Locked — hard safety

1Catalyst lockoutLocked

Hard-blocks the trade if a material SEC filing (8-K) falls inside the option's life. Earnings is different — it's a folded factor, not a veto (see Earnings mode).

A confirmed 8-K/binary ≤ DTE away vetoes; an unconfirmed one within max(DTE, 7) days. Earnings, when the expiry is in its window, does not raise catalyst:earnings_in_tenor under the default "consider" view — its news bias is folded into the score instead, and a no-go read holds the name back. (Only the legacy "veto" view hard-blocks earnings.)

Tokens: catalyst:{kind}_in_tenor (8-K etc.), catalyst:unconfirmed_{kind}

2Liquidity floorLocked

Refuses spreads you can't get out of cheaply: the package bid/ask must be tight, both legs deep in open interest, and actually quoted with size.

Package spread must be ≤ 8% of the credit; each leg needs ≥ 250 open interest (raised to 1000 for heavy names like NVDA/TSLA); there must be size at the touch. A zero/negative credit or an unquoted package also fails.

Tokens: liquidity:no_credit, liquidity:spread_pct_of_credit, liquidity:open_interest, liquidity:size_at_touch, liquidity:unquoted (unknown)

3Dollar-friction testLocked

The dollar edge must be at least 2× the round-trip commission — a check that DTE alone can't express, because fees are fixed in dollars while the edge shrinks with time.

Round-trip cost ≈ 4 × the per-leg one-way commission for a two-leg vertical (default $0.35/leg). If gross EV in dollars is below 2× that, it fails.

Tokens: dollar_friction, friction:unavailable (unknown)

4Chain sanityLocked

A sanity check on the data itself — not the trade. Both put and call sides present, spot inside the listed strikes, no crossed/locked quotes, greeks present.

This is the one that most often fires as a transient provider hiccup (e.g. Yahoo returns a one-sided chain) rather than a market signal — it usually clears on the next sweep.

Tokens: chain_sanity:missing_side, :spot_outside_chain, :crossed_market, :greeks_missing, :no_chain

5Macro calendar validityLocked

Refuses any expiry that reaches past the macro-calendar table's coverage — better to fail visibly than to silently sell through a CPI or FOMC print the engine can't see.

If the expiry is beyond the table's valid_through date → fail. If the coverage number is missing → unknown. See Catalysts for the table itself.

Tokens: macro_calendar:expiry_beyond_valid_through, macro_calendar:unavailable (unknown)

Tunable — your knobs

6DTE windowTunable

Only trade expiries inside the allowed days-to-expiration band.

Default 7–45 DTE. Your settings clamp min_dte to 1–120 and max_dte to 1–180 (with max > min).

Token: dte_window

7IV percentile floorTunable

Implied vol must be rich enough by its own 1-year percentile before selling premium is worth it.

Default floor 40 (IV percentile, i.e. how often the past year was cheaper). Your knob min_iv_percentile spans 0–100. No IV history → unknown.

Tokens: iv_percentile_floor, volatility_history_unavailable (unknown)

8Vol risk premium floorTunable

Implied vol must exceed realized vol by a minimum ratio (not vol points), and the two realized-vol estimators must agree so the "edge" isn't just an overnight-gap artifact.

Default ratio floor 1.15 (IV ÷ Yang-Zhang realized vol). If the two RV estimators disagree beyond tolerance (0.35), it flags rv_estimator_disagreement instead.

Tokens: vrp_floor, rv_estimator_disagreement, vrp_unavailable (unknown)

9Short-delta bandTunable

The short strike's delta must sit in the sweet spot — very-low-delta (5–10Δ) shorts are measurably negative-EV, so they're refused.

Default band 0.20–0.35 (target 0.275). Your clamps keep both bounds inside 0.15–0.40.

Tokens: short_delta_band, short_delta:unavailable (unknown)

10Structure eligibilityTunable

After everything above, at least one concrete spread structure must have survived — if none did, there is simply nothing to sell.

Fires when no structure fits the name/regime (no_eligible_structure), all candidates died in the per-strike gates (no_candidate), or the product is excluded (ineligible:…, e.g. an index product).

Tokens: no_eligible_structure, no_candidate, ineligible:{reason}

Also present — soft guards (not among the 10):

Squeeze veto, sentiment veto and sector-dispersion are separate toggleable guards. They surface as ⚠ avoid_if warnings or an "override is off" banner on the card, not as checklist gates.

The tradeability score

When a name clears the gates, its 0–100 score is a weighted blend, then multiplied by the market-regime multiplier. The weights encode the priority order: can you get out safely first, edge second, liquidity third.

ComponentWeightWhat it rewards
Structural safety0.30Short strike beyond the dealer wall, wall strength, favorable gamma-flip side.
Expected-move headroom0.20Short strike sitting comfortably beyond the expected move (in σ units).
Volatility richness0.20IV percentile/rank + IV/RV ratio + term-structure slope.
Credit quality0.15Alpha (EV/max-loss) + credit-to-width efficiency + POP.
Liquidity quality0.10Tight spread (in IV points) + OI depth + size at touch.
Sentiment lean0.05Side selection / suppression only — never a promoter.

Confidence (shown under the bar) is a separate 0–1 data-quality read — it degrades a candidate when inputs are thin, but never blocks it. Regime (contango / transition / backwardation / stressed) sets the score multiplier and can suppress structures.

Catalysts: earnings & the macro calendar

Earnings blackout

The catalyst gate blocks trading over earnings, but how much it blocks depends on how sure it is of the date:

  • Confirmed & cross-checked (≥ 2 sources agree) → block the single report day, extended one extra day for an after-hours report (it moves the next session).
  • Unconfirmed, single-source, or sources disagree → block the entire Mon–Fri trading week spanning the candidate dates. A lone vendor is not a cross-check.

SEC 8-K via EDGAR is the hard source: the backend watches EDGAR's near-real-time feed and treats an Item 2.02 filing ("results of operations") as ground truth that earnings happened — it's used to repair/confirm vendor dates. Material filings (e.g. NT 10-Q) inside the tenor also veto. Anything in EDGAR is confirmed = true — not a guess.

Nasdaq cross-check is an optional free source, off unless SIMMER_EARNINGS_NASDAQ is set. It never confirms a single day (Nasdaq exposes no confirmed flag) — it can only widen a blackout. "No blackout" is only trusted as safe when ≥ 2 sources actually answered.

Earnings-VRP (a soft warning)

The engine splits the vol-risk premium into an earnings-driven piece and an ex-earnings baseline. If total VRP is positive but the ex-earnings piece is negative, the "edge" is really an earnings event sitting just outside the trade window — it catches what the hard gate misses. It raises the note premium_entirely_earnings_driven (an avoid_if, not a veto).

Earnings mode — a folded factor, not a veto

When the viewed expiry sits in an earnings window, Simmer does not hard-veto. It runs the standalone earnings analyzer (Alpaca news → Gemini bias, cached) and folds that read into the score. Source: SIMMER_EARNINGS_PROVIDER=yahoo supplies the next earnings date (SEC only confirms past earnings). Three views:

ViewWhat it does
consider (default — what the card shows)Bias folded: a go + aligned read lifts the score (±15, confidence-scaled); opposed penalizes; a no-go holds the name back — it can never reach "ready" (earnings cuts both ways). No hard earnings veto.
ignoreEarnings treated as absent — no veto, no fold. The pure ex-earnings structural/VRP decision ("what would this be if earnings weren't happening").
vetoLegacy hard block. Not used by the card; available for explicit conservative runs.

The card toggle (CONSIDER ↔ IGNORE)

The engine attaches both verdicts to the envelope (earnings + earnings_alt), so the toggle is a pure display swap — no re-analysis, no engine retrigger. The choice persists per symbol in the browser's localStorage (survives refresh; per-device, not synced). Bias (direction/confidence/go) is computed on demand once via /simmer/earnings/{sym} and cached ~6h.

Direction is for the RUN-UP, not the gap

The analyzer's news bias forecasts pre-print drift, not the post-earnings gap (bullish news is often already priced → "sell the news"). So a ready earnings name flags close_before_print: it's a sell-the-run-up, close BEFORE the announcement play — never hold a credit spread through the print. The analyzer's real value is the no-go (avoid selling into a guidance-cut / scandal narrative), and side selection (bullish → bull put, bearish → bear call). Simmer is not a direction forecaster — see docs/simmer_earnings.md for the phased plan.

macro_calendar.json

A deliberately hand-maintained table of scheduled macro events (no live macro feed — it's a small, auditable file). It sits at market/backend/app/macro_calendar.json; override the path with SIMMER_MACRO_CALENDAR.

Shape, in plain terms:

  • valid_through — the last date the table covers. The engine refuses to score any expiry past this (gate #5): an empty event list beyond the table is "unknown", not "clear".
  • confirmed_through — last agency-verified date; rows past it are derived estimates.
  • events[] — each row: date, time_et, event (CPI, FOMC, NFP, PPI, PCE…), severity (high = hard block, medium = down-weight, low = informational), and confirmed. Derived rows carry uncertainty_days — how far the blocking window widens on each side.

A startup freshness check refuses to boot the service if the table's valid_through is too near — a stale calendar becomes a visible refusal, never a silent "no catalyst found".

Configuration

Four surfaces, by purpose. How the engine decides lives in code (overridable by a JSON file); where data comes from in the backend config file; your preferences in Supabase.

WhatWhere
Engine tunables — weights, gate thresholds, cadence, TTLs, universe market/backend/app/simmer_config.py (defaults), overridable per-section by simmer_tickers.json / $SIMMER_TICKERS_CONFIG.
Providers & keys — data source, news, Alpaca/Gemini keys, DEVMODE edgelane_market.config (KEY=VALUE, gitignored) → parsed by config.py.
Plain env flags — Nasdaq earnings, macro-table path, tickers-config path SIMMER_EARNINGS_NASDAQ, SIMMER_MACRO_CALENDAR, SIMMER_TICKERS_CONFIG.
Per-user preferences — DTE/IV/delta bounds, risk profile, sweep interval Supabase simmer_settings (migration 0010/0011), edited via the API/UI or the supabase_admin.py CLI.

Sweep interval (UI/CLI-controlled)

The watcher's open-market cadence is simmer_settings.sweep_interval (minutes, 1–60). The watcher reads the minimum across all rows each cycle (one global loop, so the tightest requested cadence wins), falling back to the 5-minute config default. A change applies on the next sweep; a restart picks it up immediately. Set it with the admin CLI:

python3 tools/supabase_admin.py update simmer_settings \
        --set sweep_interval=3 --where user_id=<uuid>

There is no DuckDB mirror of settings — the watcher reads Supabase live, so nothing caches to go stale.

DEVMODE — check before any prod backend deploy

DEVMODE=true flips the Tradier token/base/account and the DuckDB file to sandbox in one switch — a prod container with it on serves sandbox quotes while looking perfectly alive. It must be false in production.

Deploy & update

Do only the steps for what changed; the sequence matters — schema, then backend, then frontends — because the backend restart rotates the tunnel and republishes the URL pointers.

  1. DB schema changed → make db-push (applies supabase/migrations).
  2. Backend / engine changed → confirm DEVMODE=false, then make deploy-be (full, rotates the tunnel) or make deploy-be-restart (rebuilds & recreates only the backend container — tunnel untouched). A config-only change just needs a restart (the file is bind-mounted).
  3. Matrix UI changed → make deploy-fe (also re-syncs Supabase site_url + redirect allow-list).
  4. Simmer UI changed → make deploy-simmer (npm ci + build can take > 2 min).
  5. Always finish → make check-tunnel — container health, the Supabase pointer, the Edge Config pointer matching it, tunnel /status, CORS, and the auth path.

One-time setup

make frontend-setup links both Vercel projects, provisions the Edge Config self-heal, and syncs the Supabase auth allow-list. Run vercel login first (setup reads that session). For the container to self-heal the rotating tunnel URL into Edge Config, put a no-expiration VERCEL_API_TOKEN in deploy/.env — the container can't read your host CLI login.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat it means / fix
A card stuck on chain sanity · missing side The provider returned a one-sided chain (no puts or no calls) — a data veto, not a market signal. Usually transient; clears next sweep. If it persists for one name, the provider is returning a bad chain for it.
Everything is vetoed / a very quiet board Expected. Simmer is refusal-first; premium is only sellable in specific conditions. The chips' x/10 tell you which gates.
A freshly added ticker shows "no analysis yet" / "pending" The first sweep hasn't persisted a row yet (a few minutes; off-hours it uses last session's data). On-demand /simmer/analyze does not persist — to fill a card immediately, admin POST /simmer/research/{sym}/refresh?expiration=YYYY-MM-DD (writes a readiness row).
Earnings toggle "doesn't do anything" / resets on refresh By design the card already shows the consider view (earnings folded, no catalyst-lockout veto). The CONSIDER↔IGNORE toggle swaps the displayed verdict (both are on the envelope) and persists per symbol in localStorage — per browser, so a different device won't see it. It never re-runs the engine.
An in-earnings name still shows catalyst · earnings in tenor Stale persisted row from before earnings mode, or the sweep hasn't re-run. Earnings no longer hard-vetoes under "consider"; force it with the admin research-refresh above, or wait for the next sweep.
A pinned expiry passed — does the card die? No. At the top of each sweep, expired pinned expirations auto-roll to the next listed date (de-duped — if you already watch that date, the expired row is removed). A pinned ticker keeps rolling forward until you remove it; the engine never runs on a dead contract.
Simmer prod login fails / calls its own origin The app isn't resolving the backend via /api/config. Confirm it's a production build, that /api/config returns the tunnel URL, and Edge Config has api_base.
Simmer subroutes 404 on refresh (/settings, /news) SPA fallback. simmer/ui/vercel.json must use rewrites: /(.*) → /index.html and no cleanUrls; Vercel's source is path-to-regexp — no lookaheads.
After a restart, Simmer can't reach the backend (Matrix is fine) Edge Config api_base went stale — the container couldn't PATCH it. Missing/expired permanent VERCEL_API_TOKEN in deploy/.env; check the cloudflared logs and refresh it.
make check-tunnel says "Edge Config not configured" Set VERCEL_API_TOKEN + EDGE_CONFIG_ID in deploy/.env (run make frontend-setup).
Simmer email confirmation / reset link rejected on redirect The Simmer origin isn't in Supabase uri_allow_list. Run make frontend-setup or make deploy-fe.
